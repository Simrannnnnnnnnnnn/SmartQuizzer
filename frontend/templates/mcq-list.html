Question 1
Which principle of Relativity states that the laws of physics are the same for all observers in uniform motion?
A) General Relativity
B) Special Relativity
C) Quantum Mechanics
D) Classical Mechanics

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Special Relativity, proposed by Einstein in 1905, deals with observers in uniform (constant speed) motion.
